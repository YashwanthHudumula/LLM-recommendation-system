# Title-page fields required before submission

Do not add these to the anonymized review manuscript. Complete them in a separate title page or in the submission portal:

- Full author names in publication order
- Institutional affiliations and postal addresses
- Corresponding-author name and email
- ORCID identifiers
- CRediT contribution statement
- Funding organization and grant number, or an explicit no-funding statement
- Complete competing-interest declaration
- Acknowledgments
- Public repository URL and archived release DOI
- Ethics/IRB determination if required by the institution
- Confirmation of the final generative-AI disclosure against the journal policy
- Three to five suggested reviewers and any opposed reviewers, if requested

Before upload, verify the current Information Processing & Management submission checklist, abstract and keyword limits, anonymization requirements, and accepted supplementary-file formats in the live editorial system.
